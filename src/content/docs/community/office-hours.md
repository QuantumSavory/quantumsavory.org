# Office Hours

Quantum Information Science Office Hours are online sessions for questions about QuantumSavory and related Julia quantum packages, including QuantumClifford.jl, QuantumSymbolics.jl, Gabs.jl, QuantumOptics.jl, and adjacent tools.

Office hours can be [scheduled on-demand](https://cal.com/krastanov/quantum-savory-office-hours).

Hosted by Prof. Stefan Krastanov and Hana KimLee.

Good topics for office hours include:

- choosing a backend for a simulation,
- getting started with a package,
- discussing a bounty issue,
- debugging a small example,
- checking whether a proposed contribution fits a package.
